What a purchase costs

Transfer fees
3% to 8% on a rising scale, reduced by half where no VAT is charged. Nothing to pay where VAT is.
VAT
19% on a new build. 5% on the first 130 square metres of a main home, within value and area limits, and held for ten years.
Annual tax
No national property tax since 2017. Municipal rates and sewerage only.
On sale
Capital gains at 20% on the gain, after lifetime allowances.
Residency
Permanent residency from a €300,000 new-build purchase, with income and family criteria.
Title
Contract and title deed are separate steps. The contract is deposited at the Land Registry to secure the buyer.

Rates and thresholds as at October 2026. They change, and they turn on your circumstances. Take advice before you rely on them.
