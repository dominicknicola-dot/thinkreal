What a purchase costs in Dubai

Transfer
4% to the Dubai Land Department, split with the seller in law and paid by the buyer in practice, plus registration and agency.
Where you may buy
Foreign freehold is confined to designated areas. Dubai has more than forty.
Annual cost
No annual property tax and no personal income tax on rent. Service charges instead, per square foot, set by the community.
Residency
A ten-year Golden Visa from AED 2 million of property.

Rates and thresholds as at October 2026, and the Emirates is not one jurisdiction: each one legislates for itself. Take advice before you rely on them. Imagery represents the region and does not depict a specific Think Real property.
